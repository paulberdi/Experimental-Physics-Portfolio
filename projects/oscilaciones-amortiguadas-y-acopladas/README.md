# Oscilaciones amortiguadas y acopladas

Experimental physics project associated with the study of damped and coupled oscillatory systems in FIS200 at Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María (UTFSM).

The final academic report is stored in the repository's [`papers/`](../../papers/) directory. This project folder contains the supporting experimental workbook, raw VCM measurements and photographs of the experimental setup.

## Final report

- [`Análisis experimental del comportamiento dinámico de sistemas oscilatorios amortiguados y acoplados`](../../papers/analisis-experimental-del-comportamiento-dinamico-de-sistemas-oscilatorios-amortiguados-y-acoplados.pdf)

## Contents

- [`data/experimental-data.xlsx`](./data/experimental-data.xlsx) — experimental workbook.
- [`data/raw/`](./data/raw/) — original VCM measurement files.
- [`figures/setup-1.jpg`](./figures/setup-1.jpg) and [`figures/setup-2.jpg`](./figures/setup-2.jpg) — photographs of the experimental setup.

## Data organization

The VCM files are preserved as raw experimental measurements and renamed consistently without assigning additional physical meaning to individual runs beyond what is documented in the original files.
